Key Insights
1. The Illusion of High Recall
At first glance, MM-Det appears to have an outstanding Fake Recall, ranging from 88% to 100% across all 20 tested architectures. However, this metric is highly misleading when paired with the ROC-AUC scores.

2. Complete Lack of Discriminative Power (Random Guessing)
The ROC-AUC scores for MM-Det are catastrophic. Almost all generators score between 0.48 and 0.63, which is statistically equivalent to random guessing (0.50). This perfectly corroborates the earlier finding that MM-Det simply classifies the vast majority of all videos (both real and fake) as "fake." The model lacks genuine underlying discriminative features and operates with a severe false-positive bias.

3. Actively Incorrect on AnimateDiff
The most alarming data point is AnimateDiff (T2V), where the ROC-AUC drops to 0.3916. An AUC below 0.50 indicates that the model is actively assigning higher fake probabilities to real videos than it is to the fake AnimateDiff videos. This suggests a fundamental failure in the model's feature reconstruction pipeline when processing this specific type of temporal data.

Observation:
AIGVDet shows strong generator-dependent behavior. It is highly conservative overall: it almost never misclassifies real videos as fake, but it misses many fake videos. Generator-level results show that this low fake recall is concentrated in several I2V and V2V categories, especially SEINE, SVD, Open-Sora, LTX, and Pyramid-Flow variants. In contrast, it performs much better on several T2V generators such as AnimateDiff, Wan2.1, AccVideo, HunyuanVideo, IPOC, and VideoCrafter.

Another important pattern is that the RGB branch contributes much more than the optical-flow branch for many generators. For several fake generators, the optical-flow score is nearly zero while the RGB score is much higher. This suggests that, under this AIGVDBench setting, AIGVDet's temporal/optical-flow branch may not transfer as well as its spatial RGB branch. The fused 0.5/0.5 score therefore becomes conservative and often falls below the 0.5 fake threshold, leading to low recall despite moderate-to-good threshold-free AUC.
